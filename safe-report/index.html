    Don't trust it. Check it.

    You're about to paste something that could cost someone their job, so you should be suspicious. Three ways to prove nothing leaves your computer:

    1. Turn off your wifi

    Open this page, turn your wifi off (or go somewhere with no signal at sea), then use it. It works exactly the same, because your story never needs to go anywhere.

    2. Watch the network

    Press F12 (on a Mac: Cmd + Option + I) and open the Network tab. Paste your story and press every button. Nothing new shows up. The page also blocks itself from sending anything, with a security setting (Content Security Policy) set to connect-src 'none'.

    3. Read the code

    The whole tool is one file of plain, readable code. Nothing hidden, nothing scrambled. Hand it to your company's IT person.

    What we collect

    Nothing. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. Your browser only remembers whether you picked light or dark mode. Never the text you paste.

    What it can't do

    Safe Report catches the common patterns: vessel names after MV, MT, MS and similar, quoted ship names, IMO and MMSI numbers, call signs, names after a rank, ports after words like "arrived at" or "bound for", positions, dates, times, phones and emails. It can miss unusual names, nicknames, and details that only fit one ship or one person. Always read the anonymous version before you share it. Your company's reporting rules still come first.
