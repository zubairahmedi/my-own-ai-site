About

Zehaan Khan

Founder of ZehaanAI. Greater Toronto Area.

Zehaan Khan is the founder of ZehaanAI. He builds the systems that sit between a franchise inquiry and an intro call: the first touch, the follow-up, one CRM for every lead source, a dashboard with the full data, and an AI RevOps Engine that reads all of it.

He has been building AI automation since 2023 and franchise lead systems since 2024. He's a certified Retell AI partner, which you can see on his LinkedIn profile.

Who ZehaanAI works with

Franchise development teams in the US and Canada: the people who work candidates from the first inquiry to a booked intro call. The work so far has been for a franchise consulting firm working consultant network leads, and the same problems show up in franchisor pipelines. Leads go unanswered, follow-up depends on someone remembering, every source lives in a different place, and nobody can say where the money is leaking.

What he has built

  • An AI voice agent for franchise leads on Retell AI and GoHighLevel. It has placed 5,379 calls and worked 1,733 unique leads, reaching a live person 37% of the time. Every answer becomes a CRM field. How it was built, including what went wrong.
  • The full lead system for a franchise consulting firm: every source in one CRM, automatic follow-up and a dashboard split by consultant. Booked appointments went from an average of 89 a month to 163, and show rate from 32% to 41%. Consultant network leads, before and after. Other things changed too. Read the case study.
  • An AI RevOps Engine that runs on that firm's live data and answers questions like "where are we losing money?" with the number and the list behind it. What it is and what it finds.
  • Three free tools for franchise development teams: the Franchise Website Grader, the CRM Leak Scanner and the cost per sale calculator.
  • The complete guide to franchise lead follow-up with AI, written from those 5,379 calls.
