Questions

What should a franchise development website include?

An inquiry form, a phone number, the investment range, who you are looking for, open territories, the steps to ownership, proof from owners, and consent wording near the form.

Does the grader save my results?

No. It reads the public pages once and shows you the result. It does not store the pages or the score.

Why did something get marked as missing when it is on my site?

It reads your page and a few pages it links to, and it can miss things that load after a page opens. Enter your exact franchise page for the fairest result.

Should a franchise website show the investment range?

It helps serious candidates qualify themselves. In the 2025 ServiceScore mystery shop, 38 of 101 sites showed Item 7 investment information. Have your franchise attorney review what you publish.
