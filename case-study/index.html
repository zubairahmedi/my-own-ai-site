Case study

From 89 to 163 booked appointments a month

A franchise consulting firm · GoHighLevel + Retell AI · client anonymized

A franchise consulting firm was paying for leads from ads, website forms and third-party feeds. The leads were coming in, but too many went cold. We rebuilt what happens between the inquiry and the booked meeting.

Consultant network leads, before and after. Other things changed too. This is one firm, not a franchisor. We compare monthly averages, and we can't credit the AI alone.

What we don't claim

  • We don't claim every lead got called. The AI worked 1,733 unique leads, while roughly 750 came in each month.
  • We don't compare lead volume or conversion rate. The 2024 numbers include an initial CRM import, which would flatter the result.
  • We don't publish an average speed. We haven't finished matching every first dial to the time the lead was created.
  • We don't credit the AI alone. Other things changed over those 20 months too.
  • We don't quote deals or earnings. Appointments and show rate are what we measured.

How the numbers were pulled

Call figures come from the live calling platform record, January 6, 2025 to September 26, 2026. Before and after figures come from the CRM, across about 24,000 contacts and every calendar event, as per-month averages.
