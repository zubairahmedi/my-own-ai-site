What happened

We rebuilt what happens after the inquiry.

A franchise consulting firm had leads coming in from Facebook ads, 18 website forms, email and third-party feeds. Good candidates were going cold because a rep was busy or a follow-up never fired.

We put every source into one CRM, added an AI voice agent that calls, qualifies and books, and let the follow-up run on its own. Then we built a dashboard with the full data and pointed an AI at all of it.

Booked appointments went from an average of 89 a month to 163. Show rate went from 32% to 41%.

Questions

The straight answers.

What does franchise AI actually do?

It handles the work between a franchise inquiry and an intro call: the first touch, the follow-up, getting every source into one CRM, and showing where candidates went cold. Your development team still has every real sales conversation.

Does this replace our development team?

No. The system qualifies and schedules. It never sells, negotiates or discusses the investment. People do that part.

Which CRMs does it work with?

The case study ran on GoHighLevel, and we also work with HubSpot. FranConnect is a poor fit today because its API is gated and it ships its own AI.

Can the AI call every lead within a minute?

No, and be careful with anyone who says yes. AI voice calls need consent that covers AI voice from your brand, and calls have to stay inside legal calling windows. Portal and broker leads should usually get an instant email and a human call task instead.

Will the AI talk about earnings?

It is built to refuse. Earnings claims belong in Item 19 of your FDD, so earnings questions go to a person.

Was the case study a franchisor?

No. It was a franchise consulting firm working consultant network leads. The same problems show up in franchisor pipelines, but the numbers are from that one firm.
