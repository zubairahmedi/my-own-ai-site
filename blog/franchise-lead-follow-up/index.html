Franchise Lead Follow-Up: A System That Doesn't Depend on Memory

By Zehaan Khan · 2026-11-08 · 4 min read

Part of our complete guide: Franchise Lead Follow-Up With AI

The short version

  • At $351 a lead and 2% to a signed deal, one touch is the expensive option.
  • Every lead needs a scheduled next step or a closed reason.
  • Cap the calls, honor opt-outs, and start old leads on email.

Franchise leads rarely go cold because a rep doesn't care. They go cold because a rep got busy, a task got buried, and nobody noticed the candidate only ever got one touch.

The fix isn't asking people to try harder. It's a follow-up system that doesn't depend on anyone remembering.

One touch is expensive

The 2026 Annual Franchise Development Report puts the average cost per lead at $351 and leads to signed deals at 2%. That means roughly 50 leads for every deal, and about 24 weeks from lead to agreement.

A long sales cycle means candidates need more than one conversation. Yet FranFunnel's study of 530 brands found 73% never sent a text at all.

Candidates who don't pick up the first call aren't necessarily out. They may just be at work.

Old leads need more care

Going back through dormant leads is the obvious move, since you've already paid for them. We haven't run this ourselves yet, so treat this section as guidance. It's also where the risk is.

Older leads may have consented under language that never mentioned AI voice. Phone numbers get reassigned. In Canada, implied consent from an inquiry only lasts 6 months.

So start reactivation with email, and only move someone to a call once they've said yes.
