Cost per franchise sale is your total development spend divided by the agreements you signed. It's the number that tells you whether your lead budget is working.

The average went from $13,757 in 2024 to $17,550 in 2025, according to the 2026 Annual Franchise Development Report. If you'd rather skip the math, use our free cost per sale calculator.

The formula

Use a full year if you can. With a sales cycle of about 24 weeks, a single month or quarter can mislead you, because the money you spend now may not produce a signed deal for months.

At the industry's 2% lead to sale rate, you need about 50 leads per deal. At $351 each, that's $17,550 in lead cost alone.

Most teams try to fix the first number by negotiating with vendors. The second number usually has more room.

Where the money leaks

You've already paid for every lead in your CRM. So the cheapest way to lower cost per sale is to work the ones you've already bought.

FranFunnel's study of 530 brands found 28% never replied to a web inquiry. In ServiceScore's 2025 mystery shop, 44% of brands called the prospect.

What we saw in practice

For the franchise consulting firm in our case study, booked appointments went from an average of 89 a month to 163. Other things changed too, and this was consultant network leads, so we don't claim a specific cost per sale change from it.

We also can't give you a clean before-and-after on lead volume. The 2024 numbers include a one-time CRM import, which would have made our result look better than it was. Check your own data for the same problem before you trust a trend.
