Before you add AI to franchise development, get every lead into one place. Without that, automation just moves a mess around faster.

It was the first thing we did for the firm in our case study. Leads came from Facebook ads, 18 website forms, email, webhooks and third-party feeds. All of it now lands in one GoHighLevel account.

Why sources shouldn't be treated the same

FranConnect's 2025 Franchise Sales Index shows how differently each source converts to a signed agreement:

A lead from inside your network and a portal lead shouldn't get the same treatment. You can only treat them differently if the CRM knows where each one came from.

The consent field is the one people skip

Portal and broker leads usually gave consent to the portal, not to your brand. That matters as soon as an AI wants to call them.

So new builds keep two paths. Our compliance guide explains why.

Three problems you'll hit

Which CRM?

We've done this on GoHighLevel, and we also work with HubSpot. FranConnect is built for franchise development, but its API is gated and it comes with its own AI, so outside systems are harder to connect.

Use whatever your team will actually keep updated. A simple CRM with clean data beats a powerful one nobody trusts.

What you get once it's done

With everything in one place, the rest becomes possible: a fast first touch, a real dashboard, and an AI that can read the whole pipeline.

Start by listing every place a lead can come from, then check whether each one reaches your CRM with all six fields. Most teams find at least one that doesn't.
