Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

ZehaanAI is run by Zehaan Khan in Ontario, Canada. Zehaan is responsible for privacy here. Questions go to enquiries@zehaanai.com.

What we collect

When you fill in our form, we collect your name, work email, franchise brand, CRM and anything you write in the notes. We also record the time and the page you sent it from.

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

Why we collect it

We use it to reply to you, run the inquiry teardown you asked for and send you the result. We won't add you to a marketing list unless you agree to it.

Who else handles it

We don't sell your information. These services are involved in running the site:

  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts delivers our fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Our automation server and email provider receive form submissions so we can reply.

Some of these services store data outside Canada.

Free tools

Our calculator and CRM Leak Scanner run in your browser. What you type, and any file you scan, stays on your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. If you ask for the scanner's PDF, we receive only the name, email and brand you enter.

Our Website Grader reads the public pages at the address you enter once and doesn't save them or the result. Our host keeps standard request logs.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for up to 24 months, unless we're working together and need them longer. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email enquiries@zehaanai.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
